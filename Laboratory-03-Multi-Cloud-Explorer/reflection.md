## My Reflections


As an IT Student, out of all three cloud providers, AWS stood out to me the most due to its extensive service offerings and operational adaptability. It easily scales to meet the demands of early-stage startups as well as massive enterprise environments.

While each platform delivers fundamental cloud capabilities like compute resources, data storage, networking, and security measures, they each excel in distinct areas. Azure dominates in Microsoft-centric setups, while GCP takes the lead in artificial intelligence and advanced analytics.

Ultimately, selecting the right cloud environment boils down to specific business objectives. A company's unique operational demands—rather than market popularity—should drive the final decision.

Through this project, I gained practical experience in evaluating cloud infrastructure and matching technical solutions to actual business requirements. I also enhanced my research capabilities and learned to write clearer technical documentation.

Overall, completing this exercise helped me structure my work far better, giving my portfolio a much more polished and professional look.
